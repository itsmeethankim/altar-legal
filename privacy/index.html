BeBible

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 27, 2026

BeBible ("we", "our", or "the app") helps you build a daily Scripture habit, guard selected apps with Apple's Screen Time tools, and share a daily photo and reflection with private, invite-only Groups. This policy explains the information the app handles, why it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

BeBible was previously called Altar. It is the same app and developer under a new name.

The short version

  • We do not sell personal information, run ads, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Your Screen Time selections, Hard Block Mode schedule, daily streak, onboarding age band, and private scan history stay on your device.
  • Sign in with Apple is required for Groups. Group posts, photos, comments, replies, membership, reports, and blocks use our Supabase and Cloudflare services.
  • Successful post photos can be saved locally for Memories and, with your permission, to a named BeBible Photos album. Feed copies in Cloudflare R2 are temporary.
  • Optional product analytics are on by default, contain only an approved set of interaction and app-context fields, and can be turned off in the app.
  • Optional AI-assisted Scripture features send text—not camera images—to OpenAI only after you turn on Scan processing in the app's Privacy settings.

Information kept on your device

Screen Time and Hard Block Mode

Your selected applications, categories, and web domains are represented by Apple Family Controls tokens. BeBible stores those tokens in its private App Group so the app and its Screen Time extensions can apply the shields you request. The selection is not uploaded to BeBible, Supabase, RevenueCat, Cloudflare, or OpenAI. Hard Block Mode schedules, temporary unlock state, and related settings also stay on the device and are handled through Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks.

Local spiritual and app data

  • Onboarding choices such as your first name, age band, and whether you chose the social or solo path. These stay local, except that the name you entered may be used as your Group display name if you later sign in. Your estimated daily screen hours are used during onboarding to calculate setup illustrations but are not retained after setup.
  • Your daily open-app streak and the date it last advanced.
  • Your settings, reading-plan progress, intervention history, scan history, unlock history, reflections, and locally bundled Bible text. Cloud Sync for this private data is not enabled in the current release.
  • Camera images and recognized text while Scan to Unlock is processing. Camera images are processed in memory and are not uploaded as part of the scan flow.
  • Successful Group captures used by local Memories. These include the two captured photos and their local metadata.

Pending post retries

If a Group post cannot finish while its Group day is still active, its two compressed photos and a small retry record may remain in app-private storage. They do not appear in Memories or the BeBible Photos album until the server confirms a post. An expired retry is hidden and is removed after the app can confirm that no post was accepted; an ambiguous offline retry may remain privately until the next reconciliation. These retry files are excluded from device backups.

Accounts and authentication

Anonymous Supabase session

Before Sign in with Apple, BeBible may create an anonymous Supabase authentication session. It has a random backend user identifier and does not contain your Apple identity. It supports authenticated connectivity, rate limiting, and pre-sign-in product analytics. Reinstalling the app may create a different anonymous identifier.

Sign in with Apple

When you use Groups, Sign in with Apple gives Supabase an app-specific identifier. Apple may also provide your name and either your email address or an Apple relay email, depending on your choices and what Apple has already shared for this app. BeBible uses the account to maintain your session, save your display name, authorize Group access, connect subscription status, apply abuse limits, deliver notifications, and support account deletion. Group members see your display name, not your email address or Apple account identifier. We never receive your Apple password.

Purchases and entitlements

Apple processes purchases and payment information. RevenueCat receives purchase and entitlement information from Apple and is linked to your BeBible backend user identifier after you sign in. BeBible and Supabase store the resulting entitlement status, expiration information, environment, update time, and RevenueCat app-user identifiers so paid access can be enforced consistently. We do not receive your full card or bank details.

Private Groups

Groups are invite-only. Free members may belong to one Group total or create one four-person referral Group. Premium members may belong to or create up to ten Groups, and a Premium-owned Group can hold up to 50 members. If a free referral Group is permanently upgraded, that capacity remains available as described in the app.

When you create, join, or use a Group, we store:

  • Your display-name profile and Community Guidelines acceptance.
  • Group name, owner, members, join times, invite code, time zone, access basis, and capacity.
  • For each successful post: author, Group, capture and post identifiers, capture time, local and Group day, optional Scripture reference and text, caption, reflection, picture-in-picture position, and the two photos.
  • Comments and one-level replies, including author, body, parent relationship, and creation time. Comments and replies are final after posting but can be deleted by their author.
  • Blocks, and reports that may include a reason and optional details.
  • Rate-limit counters used to protect feed, post, comment, report, and account actions from abuse.

Members of the same Group can see its roster and each other's display names. After posting for that Group's current day, a member can see the day's visible posts, photos, shared Scripture and reflection content, comments, and replies. Members cannot see your email, Apple account identifier, Screen Time selections, other Groups, private local history, or content hidden by a block or report rule.

Reports and blocks

Reporting a post, comment, or reply hides it for the reporter. Reports from two distinct members hide the content from the Group pending review. The reported author is not notified. Blocking prevents both people from seeing each other's posts, comments, replies, profiles, and related notifications in shared Groups; factual membership may remain visible to Group owners for management. The blocked person is not notified.

Group ownership and leaving

Leaving a Group removes your access but does not delete the posts and comments you previously shared there; remaining members retain access according to the normal Group rules. If an owner leaves a Group with other members, ownership transfers to the earliest remaining member. If the last member leaves, the Group and its related server content are deleted. Removing a member has the same access effect as leaving.

Photo storage and Memories

  • Cloudflare R2: the two JPEGs for a Group post are uploaded to private object storage under a random capture identifier. Upload links expire after about 15 minutes and authorized feed-download links after about two minutes. A bucket lifecycle rule deletes objects after one day; because lifecycle cleanup is periodic, actual deletion can occur roughly 24–48 hours after upload.
  • App-private Memories: after at least one post is confirmed, BeBible keeps the captured pair and its local record on your device so it can appear in Memories. Other members' current-day feed images may be cached locally and are cleared when the Group day changes.
  • BeBible Photos album: if you grant full Photos read/write access, BeBible creates or finds an album named BeBible Photos and saves each confirmed pair with filenames containing the capture identifier. BeBible limits its recovery work to exact albums with that name and matching filenames; it does not use this feature to scan unrelated albums.

Deleting a post removes its server capture and every per-Group post and thread attached to that capture. BeBible also requests deletion of its R2 objects, app-private files, cached copies, and matching BeBible Photos assets. R2 cleanup is best effort because the lifecycle rule is the final deletion boundary. Photos cleanup requires current permission and Apple's confirmation; if it cannot complete, an album copy may remain on your device, but BeBible ignores it when no matching server post exists. Deleting your account does not delete recovery copies already saved in Apple Photos.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Apple gives BeBible an APNs device token. We store the token with your backend user identifier, platform, app environment, bundle identifier, and last-seen time so we can send Group post, comment, and reply alerts and route a tap to the related content. Notification delivery goes through Apple Push Notification service. Tokens are removed when you sign out or delete your account when that cleanup can be completed; Apple may rotate or invalidate them independently. BeBible also schedules selected reminders locally on your device.

AI-assisted Scripture features

Scan to Unlock first processes the camera image on your device with Apple Vision OCR. It then compares the recognized words with Bible text bundled in the app. The camera image itself is not sent to Supabase or OpenAI. When on-device matching cannot confidently identify the page, the optional AI identification fallback may send the recognized OCR text to our Supabase Edge Function and then to OpenAI.

Other optional AI requests use text only:

  • The devotional generator sends the confirmed Scripture reference. For a manually typed passage, this is the reference you typed.
  • The Bible Assistant sends the question you type and may include the confirmed Scripture reference and the recognized verse text currently shown in the scan flow.
  • Your written reflection is not included in these AI requests. A devotional generated by OpenAI may be saved locally as the scan reflection if you do not write your own, but it is not sent back to OpenAI as reflection input.
  • The current scan interface does not attach your name, email, backend user identifier, location, Screen Time selections, Group content, or onboarding age band to the OpenAI payload.

Consent defaults to off. The current scan flow does not interrupt the camera with a separate AI warning. Instead, the explicit consent control is the Profile → Account → Privacy → Scan processing toggle, whose label explains that scanned text is sent to OpenAI. This one toggle controls all AI-assisted Scripture features, including the Bible Assistant. The choice is stored for the device and currently remains in effect if you sign out or change accounts on that device. You can turn it off at any time. While it is off, OCR, passage matching, and fallback devotional responses remain on the device, and BeBible does not make an OpenAI request. AI Assistant access is also hidden while consent is off.

When consent is on and you use an eligible AI feature, Supabase verifies your signed-in account and Premium entitlement, applies per-user, per-IP, and global limits, and forwards the permitted text to OpenAI's /v1/chat/completions API. Supabase stores your backend account identifier, request time, prompt character count, and status in an assistant request log; it does not store the prompt body in that table. Those account-linked request rows are deleted when the account is deleted. A separate abuse-control table stores the source IP address, rolling request count, window start, and last-request time. Its count resets after a 24-hour window, but the database currently has no automatic schedule that deletes the IP row.

OpenAI may retain API prompts and responses in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days, unless longer retention is required by law or is reasonably necessary to protect OpenAI's services, users, or third parties from harm. OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default unless the API account owner explicitly opts in to data sharing. BeBible does not request persistent Chat Completions application state. See OpenAI API data controls.

OpenAI returns generated text to BeBible. AI responses may be inaccurate or theologically imprecise and are not professional spiritual, medical, legal, or financial advice. If you choose Report on an AI response, BeBible opens your email app with a portion of that response and space for your explanation; sending the email is your choice and is then handled by your email provider.

Product analytics

BeBible sends a limited set of product-interaction events to its own Supabase backend so we can understand onboarding, paywall, Screen Time, scan, subscription, Group, social, Memories, and Hard Block Mode flows. Analytics are on by default and can be disabled under Profile → Account → Privacy → Help improve BeBible.

Analytics may include an event name, approved scalar properties such as a step or action category, event time, a random install identifier, a per-launch identifier, app version, build number, platform, environment, and whether Premium was active. After sign-in, the event is also associated with your Supabase user identifier. The analytics boundary rejects unapproved properties: it does not send invite codes, Group or post identifiers, names, authored posts, comments, reflections, prayers, scanned Scripture text, photos, or Screen Time selections.

Events may be queued in app-private storage before upload. Turning analytics off stops new uploads and clears the local queue. Uploaded analytics events are automatically deleted after 90 days. Account deletion removes analytics attached to the signed-in account; pre-sign-in events attached to a separate anonymous session expire under the same 90-day rule.

How we use information

  • Provide authentication, private Groups, daily posts, comments, replies, Memories, Screen Time guards, Scripture tools, and notifications.
  • Verify purchases and enforce free and Premium Group limits.
  • Prevent abuse, enforce Community Guidelines, and investigate reports.
  • Operate optional AI-assisted features after consent.
  • Understand app reliability and product flows through bounded analytics.

Retention and deletion

  • Group rows, capture metadata, posts, comments, replies, membership, and account data remain until the related content, Group, or account is deleted, except where leaving a Group preserves previously shared content for remaining members.
  • R2 feed photos follow the one-day lifecycle described above. Short-lived signed URLs expire much sooner.
  • Analytics events are deleted after 90 days.
  • AI request metadata tied to your account remains until account deletion. IP rate-limit rows currently have no automatic deletion schedule, although their rolling request count resets after 24 hours. OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring as described above.
  • Limited report records may remain as moderation evidence after the reporter or reported content is deleted. They contain the report type, reason, optional details, reporter identifier, and time—not a retained copy of a deleted photo, post, or comment.
  • Local data remains until you delete it in the app, sign out where applicable, delete the app, or use an applicable iOS data-removal action. Signing out clears local Memories records, their app-private photo pairs, and social caches, but does not delete server posts or copies saved in Apple Photos. Apple Photos copies remain under your control.

Your choices

  • Change notification, camera, Photos, and Screen Time permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Turn off product analytics, or turn off all AI-assisted Scripture features using the Scan processing toggle, in BeBible's Privacy settings.
  • Delete your own post, comment, or reply; block or unblock another member; report content; leave a Group; or remove members when you are the owner.
  • Delete your account under Profile → Account → Delete account. This removes the Supabase authentication identity and its linked profile, memberships, posts, comments, blocks, device tokens, entitlements, and other rows that cascade from the account. Owned Groups transfer or close as described above. R2 photos expire under their lifecycle rule, limited report evidence may remain, and Apple Photos copies remain under your control.
  • Delete the app to remove app-private local data. If you used Groups, delete your account first if you also want linked server data removed.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS/TLS. Social database tables use row-level restrictions or service-only access, and feed photos use short-lived signed URLs. iOS protects local app storage according to the device's security settings. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

BeBible is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The age-band answer collected during onboarding stays on the device and is not attached to the backend account or used for advertising.

Service providers

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, StoreKit purchases, APNs notifications, Photos, Vision OCR, and Screen Time frameworks. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: authentication, database, Edge Functions, entitlement mirror, notifications, analytics, and abuse controls. See Supabase Privacy.
  • Cloudflare: temporary R2 storage for Group feed photos. See Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: subscription products, purchase status, and entitlements. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI: optional AI-assisted Scripture text processing after consent. See OpenAI Privacy Policy and API data controls.
